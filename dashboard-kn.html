How to Update Your Live Site
After making changes here, follow these steps to push to krishnukala.com
01 Make your changes in this admin panel
02 Click Save Changes (top right)
03 Click Export Site → downloads updated index.html
04 Go to github.com/krishnukala/krishnukala.github.io → Upload the new index.html → Your site updates in ~2 min
